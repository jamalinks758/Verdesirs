Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your construction project in Saint Lucia.

Do you offer construction services across all of Saint Lucia?

Yes — we are based in Gros Islet and serve every district in Saint Lucia, including Castries, Micoud, Vieux Fort, Soufrière, Dennery, Laborie, Choiseul, Anse la Raye, Canaries, and all surrounding communities. No project is too far.

How much does construction cost in Saint Lucia?

Costs vary based on project size, materials, site conditions, and scope. A full residential build is priced differently from a renovation or upgrade. We provide detailed, written quotes at no obligation — message us on WhatsApp and we will assess your needs and give you a clear estimate.

Do you offer site visits before giving a quote?

Yes. We offer site visits as part of our quoting process. After assessing your site in person, we provide a written, itemized quote. No work begins until you have that quote in hand and have approved it.

What construction services does Verdesirs offer?

We offer full residential construction, commercial construction, renovations and upgrades, and end-to-end project management across all of Saint Lucia. Whether you're building from the ground up or modernizing an existing space, we handle every phase from foundation to finishing touches.

How long does a construction project take in Saint Lucia?

Timelines depend on scope and complexity. A renovation may take a few weeks, while a full residential build typically spans several months. We provide a clear project schedule from day one and send you regular updates throughout so there are no surprises on timing.

Do you handle building permits and planning approvals?

Yes. We guide you through the full permitting and planning approval process in Saint Lucia. As your construction partner, we ensure your project meets all requirements set by local authorities before a single tool hits the ground.

Can you manage my project if I live abroad?

Absolutely. We regularly work with Caribbean diaspora clients building or renovating back home in Saint Lucia. We handle everything on the ground — site visits, contractor coordination, permits, and purchasing — and keep you informed with regular WhatsApp photo updates so you remain fully in control no matter where you are.

Why should I choose Verdesirs Consulting Inc.?

We are a Saint Lucia-rooted company with 6 years of experience delivering residential and commercial projects island-wide. We offer site visits, a written quote before work begins, transparent pricing, fast WhatsApp communication, and a team that shows up every single time. Our clients — local and Caribbean diaspora — trust us because we earn it on every project.
